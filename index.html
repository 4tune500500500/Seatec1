Project readiness audits

Find the weak points while they are still cheap to fix.

A readiness audit is designed to answer a simple question: if this system had to mobilize and work offshore tomorrow, what would stop it?

✓
Equipment readinessConfiguration, maintenance status, open defects, critical spares, tooling and consumables.
✓
InterfacesPower, hydraulics, data, fibre, deck equipment, vessel services, launch / recovery and project tooling.
✓
DocumentationManuals, drawings, certificates, test records, procedures, checklists and operating limits.
✓
Operational readinessCrew familiarity, maintenance plans, failure recovery, alarms, interlocks and contingency thinking.
Typical workflow
1
Review

Project scope, system details, equipment lists, manuals, drawings, FAT/SAT records and open punch items.

2
Challenge

Check interfaces, assumptions, failure points, missing information and areas likely to generate offshore delay.

3
Prioritize

Separate critical blockers from recommended improvements so the project team knows what needs attention first.

4
Close out

Provide a concise readiness report / punch list suitable for project tracking and client review.
